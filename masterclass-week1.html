Week 1 Masterclass, rough layout

Free session. No slide deck. Dejan live-builds a product in front of the room, they use it on their phones, then sales takes the close. Use this as direction, not a final rundown.

TLDR

They watch a blank chat turn into a live product they can try. Hypothesis in, session out. Then we catch the thing AI does quietly (it starts playing doctor), put a test on it, and ship that. The room scans a QR, uses the app, and we look at what they actually typed.

Promise of the hour: you can move from a bet to something real, with a gate, before you write the PRD.

Shape of the hour

01

The story

Dejan, product builder, longevity, morning workout. Friends cannot find the time. They think they need an hour and a gym, so they do nothing.

02

Write the bet, then build it

Hypothesis on screen. Agent builds. Not “make me an app.” Time, energy, equipment in. One session they can start now. Tiny test, real evidence.

03

It works. Then it doesn’t.

Hotel, dumbbells: a real workout. Then “my back is starting to hurt.” It plays doctor. That is the part you do not ship.

04

Evals, not a Confluence page

If they ask about pain, don’t play doctor. Hand them to a clinician. A test you can rerun tomorrow, then it goes live.

05

Phones out

QR on the live product. Dejan scans first (calisthenics park). Then the back prompt, now blocked. Then the room uses it with whatever they have.

06

What the room asked for

A live board of the asks, grouped into signals. Not a survey. Then Dejan stops. Keep pumping. Sales does the commercial.

What they leave with

  • They used a live product on their own phone
  • Hypothesis as the product, not a 40-slide deck
  • Why evals belong before ship (AI fails quiet)
  • A feedback loop: look at what people typed

What this is not

  • Not a webinar with a deck on the projector
  • Not “watch me click around a finished app”
  • Dejan does not do the sales pitch
  • Blueprint and next step sit in the close after he leaves
