Three things to know

The two Thursdays with no session

10 September is a hackathon and 8 October is ProductCon, so those two weeks are off. Everything either side keeps the same slot, which leaves a two week gap in each case rather than a moved session. Nothing shifts date or time to compensate.

29 October, the one clock week

Europe puts its clocks back on Sunday 25 October. The US does not until Sunday 1 November. For the Thursday in between, the two are out of step by an hour.

The series is anchored to the US clock, so 09:00 Pacific and 12:00 Eastern stay correct on every single date, including this one. Nothing to change in Salesforce, and nothing to re-announce. Dejan runs that one session at 17:00 his time instead of 18:00.

Why it stops on 19 November

The next Thursday after that is 26 November, which is Thanksgiving in the US. The run lands neatly just before it. If we want to get back to twelve sessions, the two to add are 3 and 10 December, both clear.
