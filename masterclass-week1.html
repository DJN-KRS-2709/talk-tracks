Free Masterclass · Week 1

From a blank chat to a product they use

No slide deck. Dejan live-builds in front of the room. They try it on their phones. Then sales takes the close.

TLDR

They watch a blank chat turn into a live product they can try. Hypothesis in, session out. Then we catch the thing AI does quietly (it starts playing doctor), put a test on it, and ship that. The room scans a QR, uses the app, and we look at what they actually typed.

Promise of the hour: you can move from a bet to something real, with a gate, before you write the PRD.

Shape of the hour

01

The story

Dejan, product builder, longevity, morning workout. Friends cannot find the time. They think they need an hour and a gym, so they do nothing.

02

Write the bet, then build it

Hypothesis on screen. Agent builds. Not “make me an app.” Time, energy, equipment in. One session they can start now.

03

It works. Then it doesn’t.

Hotel, dumbbells: a real workout. Then “my back is starting to hurt.” It plays doctor. That is the part you do not ship.

04

A test, not a Confluence page

If they ask about pain, don’t play doctor. Hand them to a clinician. A test you can rerun tomorrow, then it goes live.

05

Phones out

QR on the live product. They use it with whatever they have. Same prompt that played doctor is now blocked.

06

What people actually asked for

A live board of the asks. Not a survey. Then Dejan stops. Sales does the close.

What they leave with

  • A live product they used on their own phone
  • Hypothesis as the product, not a deck
  • Why you test before you ship (AI fails quiet)
  • Look at what people typed, don’t guess the need

What this is not

  • Not a webinar with slides on the projector
  • Not a walkthrough of a finished app
  • Dejan does not do the sales pitch
