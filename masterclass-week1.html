TLDR

They watch a blank chat turn into a live product they can try. Hypothesis in, session out. Then we catch the thing AI does quietly (it starts playing doctor), put a test on it, and ship that. The room scans a QR, uses the app, and we look at what they actually typed.

Then they steal the method. Same sentence, their world. They paste a starter into Lovable. While it builds, they ask Claude or Codex to guess the loopholes. They pick the eval they didn’t catch, put it on their product, and walk the process: hypothesis, eval, then the product. The room says whether they started or bounced, and the hole they hit. Dejan does not cook one of theirs.

Promise of the hour: you watch a hypothesis become a live product you use on your phone, then you cook your own, catch a hole, and leave with feedback from other people. That is the loop.

Shape of the hour

01

The story

Dejan, product builder, longevity, morning workout. Friends cannot find the time. They think they need an hour and a gym, so they do nothing.

02

Write the bet, then build it

Hypothesis on screen. Agent builds. Not “make me an app.” Time, energy, equipment in. One session they can start now. This is the cooking show. Dejan still does most of it.

03

It works. Then it doesn’t.

Hotel, dumbbells: a real workout. Then something that isn’t a workout: “my back is starting to hurt.” It plays doctor. That is the part you do not ship.

04

A test, not a Confluence ticket · Level 10

If they ask about pain, don’t play doctor. Hand them to a clinician. A test you can rerun at any point, then it goes live. Same prompt, two outcomes. AI didn’t crash. It sounded confident about something that wasn’t true.

05

Phones out

QR on the live product. They use it with whatever they have. Same prompt that played doctor is now blocked.

06

What people actually asked for

A live board of the asks. Not a survey. If you were the PM tonight, what is the next hypothesis from that list?

07

Now you. Same loop, your product

They write the sentence for their world. Who skips, what they think they need, what you give them from what they have right now. They open Lovable and paste a starter. While it builds, they ask any model (Claude, Codex) to guess the loopholes. They drop the best eval they didn’t catch themselves. If Lovable is done, Share from the top right, drop the link, classmates can try it. Then they add that eval to their product.

08

They share the process · Level 10

Two people unmute. Not a pitch. Hypothesis, the eval they added, then they walk us through the product. The room does not compliment. Started or bounced, and one hole they hit. That stencil leaves with them. Dejan does not take one further. Then he stops. Sales does the close.

What they leave with

  • A live product they used on their own phone
  • Their own app in Lovable, with an eval they chose
  • Hypothesis as the product, not a deck
  • Why you test before you ship (AI fails quiet)
  • Look at what people did, don’t guess the need: started or bounced, one hole
